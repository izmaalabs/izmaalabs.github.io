01 / The position today

IZMAA Labs does not sell products or collect payments on this temporary site. ChemPrep is licensed with a one-time PASS, but no prices have been adopted and checkout is not open. Tax handling, including GST where applicable, will be implemented at checkout. Because no payment is taken here, there is nothing to refund or cancel through these pages.

02 / Downloads

ChemPrep by IZMAA Labs is a desktop chemistry exam-preparation app licensed with a one-time PASS. Its installers are served from GitHub Releases, not through a checkout on this site.

TraderPulse by IZMAA Labs is UNDER DEVELOPMENT. It has no public download and no published price.

03 / When public checkout opens

If a public checkout is introduced in future, final refund and cancellation terms will be published on this page and shown at checkout before any payment is taken. No cancellation window, no-refund rule or payment mechanic is published today.

04 / Statutory consumer rights

Nothing on this page is intended to limit consumer rights that apply under law. Where a future purchase is covered by statutory protections, those protections remain unaffected.

05 / Questions

For questions about this policy, email izmaalabs@gmail.com. Contact details are on the Contact Us page.
