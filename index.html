Flagship product

ChemPrep. The craft, distilled.

One flagship product, engineered like a lab instrument — a Rust core with a fluid React surface, and every byte of your work kept local.

A question bank, an exam hall and a study partner — in one binary.

ChemPrep pairs a Rust-native core with a fast, dark-chrome interface. Everything runs on your machine: your questions, analytics, mock exams and daily reviews. It starts in milliseconds and stays out of the way while you work.

  • Six question types — MCQ, MSQ, NAT, Assertion–Reasoning, Match-the-following and rich-text Descriptive.
  • FTS5 global search — fuzzy search across subjects, chapters, topics, tags and questions, with a Ctrl+K command bar.
  • SM-2 spaced repetition — a daily review queue, fed by a Mistake Room built from your attempts.
  • Mock-exam simulator — timed, section-based exams with negative marking, focus mode and per-attempt results.
  • Deep analytics — accuracy, time distribution, weak-topic detection and long-run performance trends.
