The studio

Local by default.
Precise by design.

IZMAA Labs is a software studio building micro-SaaS products: focused desktop tools for high-stakes work — the kind where focus, privacy and speed matter more than flash. Every product is local-first: your data lives in a folder you own, not on someone else's server. No cloud, no telemetry, no data to sell.

  • Local-first Every workspace is a folder you own.
  • No account Install, open, begin. Nothing to sign up for.
  • No telemetry Nothing reports back. No analytics SDK.
  • Built for focus Quiet software that stays out of the way.
Products

Two products. One philosophy.

Different domains, same standards: native performance, quiet interfaces, and complete ownership of your data.

ChemPrep Chemistry exam preparation Available now

A complete preparation workspace for high-stakes chemistry exams: a question bank, a mock-exam simulator, deep analytics and spaced-repetition review — in one binary, entirely on your machine.

  • Six question types, from MCQ and MSQ to rich-text descriptive.
  • Timed, section-based mock exams with negative marking.
  • SM-2 spaced repetition fed by your own mistakes.
  • Accuracy, timing and weak-topic analytics over the long run.
Explore ChemPrep Windows · Linux · proprietary
TraderPulse Trading & tax management In development

A local-first workspace for traders: a trade journal, portfolio performance, and tax-ready books — built on the same offline-first foundation as ChemPrep.

  • A structured journal for trades and positions.
  • Portfolio performance and holding analysis.
  • Tax books and year-end preparation, computed locally.
