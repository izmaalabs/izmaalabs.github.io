03 / The problem

Guesswork quietly costs marks.

Most preparation tracks how much you studied. Fewer tools show exactly where a mark was lost and why.

01 Blind paper practice

Solving paper after paper without question-level feedback leaves the same gaps untouched.

02 Small screens

Short sessions on a smaller screen are useful, but the exam is a desktop; rehearse in the same form.

03 Shallow scorecards

A total score alone does not show which concepts, question types or timing patterns cost marks.

04 / Five pillars

Desktop rigour, measured precisely.

ChemPrep by IZMAA Labs is a companion to coaching, not a replacement for it. These are the parts the product is built around.

01
Desktop CBT arena

Timed, section-based mocks with negative marking, focus mode and per-attempt results. Question palette, mark-for-review and keyboard navigation are supported. No browser tab.

02
Custom quizzes and precise filters

Filter by exam name, exam year, subject, chapter, topic, question type and difficulty tag, with timer and shuffle controls.

03
Weak-area analytics

Accuracy, timing and trends at question level, kept in your workspace rather than a remote dashboard.

04
Spaced-repetition review queue

An SRS review queue brings back what you need to revisit, driven by your own attempts.

05
Coaching-agnostic companion

Built to sit alongside your coaching. ChemPrep by IZMAA Labs is practice and measurement, not a video course platform.

05 / The bank

Six question types, one bank.

ChemPrep by IZMAA Labs keeps objective and descriptive questions together, so practice can match the paper in front of you.

01 / MCQ Single answer

One correct option selected from a set of choices.

02 / MSQ Multiple select

Several correct options from the same question.

03 / NAT Numerical answer

A numeric response, with tolerance handled at scoring time.

04 / Assertion-Reason Assertion and reason

A paired statement and explanation to evaluate.

05 / Matching Matching pairs

Two columns linked by the relationship between entries.

06 / Descriptive Rich-text long form

Essay-style questions authored and reviewed with rich text.

06 / Inside the workspace

Find it, fix it, schedule it.

Tools that keep a growing question bank usable between mocks.

01
Global search

SQLite FTS5 search spans subjects, chapters, topics, tags, objective questions and descriptive questions, with fuzzy ranking and a command bar.

02
Duplicate review

Signature-based similarity detection groups likely duplicates into conflict clusters, with a review workspace and a safe merge path.

03
Study planner

Day sessions, an agenda and streaks sit alongside upcoming-exam tracking, so preparation is scheduled rather than improvised.

04
Inline rendering

LaTeX chemistry renders as MathML inside questions and explanations; diagrams and structures can be attached as images.
