Privacy Policy

This policy is written with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind. It explains what this static site handles, what it does not collect, and how to reach us about your data.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

01 / Who is the data fiduciary

IZMAA Labs is the data fiduciary for this site. Contact: izmaalabs@gmail.com.

This policy applies to https://izmaalabs.github.io/.

02 / What this static site handles

Theme preference
The only browser-stored value set by these pages is cp-theme in localStorage. It remembers dark or light theme and nothing else.
Request data
Standard request data is handled by the static host in order to serve the pages. We do not use it for analytics.
Google Fonts
Fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Those services may receive standard request data such as IP address when the fonts are fetched.
Email
If you choose to write to izmaalabs@gmail.com, the message and your email address are handled by the maintainer in order to reply.

03 / What we do not collect

This static site has no forms and no checkout today, so it does not collect payment details or account information. Customer accounts, licensing and checkout will be introduced later and will be covered by an updated policy before launch.

ChemPrep by IZMAA Labs is a desktop app licensed with a one-time PASS; TraderPulse by IZMAA Labs is UNDER DEVELOPMENT with no public download or price. Product-level data handling is described with each product.

04 / Purposes and lawful basis

  • The theme preference is stored only to show the theme you selected.
  • Standard request data is handled only to deliver static pages.
  • Google Fonts are loaded only to render typography.
  • Email you voluntarily send is used only to respond to you.

Where you send us a message, we rely on your voluntary action in sending it. Basic site delivery and the saved theme preference are necessary to provide the page experience described here.

05 / Retention

The theme preference stays in your browser until you clear it. Email sent to the contact address is retained only as long as needed to reply and keep a record of the correspondence. Future account and checkout services will publish their own retention terms before launch.

06 / Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

Subject to the Act, you may ask to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erase data no longer needed for the purpose it was collected;
  • raise a grievance about how data is handled;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights where the Act allows.

To exercise any of these rights, email izmaalabs@gmail.com with the request. We will respond in line with the Act. This policy is a statement of practice, not a certification or audit claim.

07 / Children's data

These pages are not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect children's data through them. If a guardian believes a child has sent us a message, write to the contact address so we can address it.

08 / Changes to this policy

We may revise this page as the site changes. The date at the top and bottom of this page records the latest version. If a future service begins handling additional data, this policy will be updated before that handling starts.

09 / Contact and grievance channel

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or a grievance, write to izmaalabs@gmail.com. Questions can be sent to the same email address.
